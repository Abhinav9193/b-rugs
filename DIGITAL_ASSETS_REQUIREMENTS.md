## 🎬 Required 5 Videos for Manufacturing Process Page

To ensure zero repetition with the Heritage Hero page, **5 brand-new dedicated video clips and matching thumbnails** are required for each step of the manufacturing journey:

---

### 1. Stage 01: DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT
- **Video File Name**: `mfg_01_design.mp4`
- **Thumbnail File Name**: `mfg_01_design.jpg`
- **Dimensions**: **1920 × 1080 px (16:9 Landscape)**
- **Duration**: 15–30 seconds loopable
- **Creative Content & Visual Description**:
  - **Scene**: Textile designer / master artist creating graph design paper (*Naksha / Talim*) or drafting rugs in CAD software.
  - **Focal Action**: Close-up of hands drawing intricate floral or geometric hand-knotted rug patterns, using scale rulers, pencil drafting on grid paper, and matching wool yarn color tufts.
  - **Lighting & Mood**: Warm, well-lit studio ambiance with natural light, focusing on the meticulous precision required before weaving begins.

---

### 2. Stage 02: YARN PREPARATION
- **Video File Name**: `mfg_02_yarn.mp4`
- **Thumbnail File Name**: `mfg_02_yarn.jpg`
- **Dimensions**: **1920 × 1080 px (16:9 Landscape)**
- **Duration**: 15–30 seconds loopable
- **Creative Content & Visual Description**:
  - **Scene**: Raw wool/silk sorting, spinning on traditional *charkha* wheels, or winding dyed hanks into spools/skeins.
  - **Focal Action**: Artisan winding colorful yarn threads around wooden reels, testing fiber tensile strength, or organizing rich botanical dye skeins on bamboo racks.
  - **Lighting & Mood**: Authentic craft ambiance, vibrant wool colors (indigo, ochre, terracotta, ivory), natural sunlight catching airborne wool texture.

---

### 3. Stage 03: WEAVING
- **Video File Name**: `mfg_03_weaving.mp4`
- **Thumbnail File Name**: `mfg_03_weaving.jpg`
- **Dimensions**: **1920 × 1080 px (16:9 Landscape)**
- **Duration**: 15–30 seconds loopable
- **Creative Content & Visual Description**:
  - **Scene**: Master weavers at vertical wooden loom structures with tight cotton/silk warp threads.
  - **Focal Action**: Rapid, rhythmic artisan hand movements tying individual Senneh or Persian knots, cutting with curved weaver knife (*churi*), and beating each row down firmly with the iron comb (*panja*).
  - **Lighting & Mood**: Cinematic depth of field, focused facial expressions of experienced artisans, detailed macro shots of thousands of knots aligning into patterns.

---

### 4. Stage 04: FINISHING & CARE
- **Video File Name**: `mfg_04_finishing.mp4`
- **Thumbnail File Name**: `mfg_04_finishing.jpg`
- **Dimensions**: **1920 × 1080 px (16:9 Landscape)**
- **Duration**: 15–30 seconds loopable
- **Creative Content & Visual Description**:
  - **Scene**: Washing, hand-embossing, shearing, and fringe edging.
  - **Focal Action**: Artisans using specialized wide scissors (*kainchi*) for precise pile carving/shearing, washing rugs with wooden squeegees and natural saponins on washing floors, edge binding (serging), and sunlight drying.
  - **Lighting & Mood**: Sparkling clean water flow, rich tactile textures of freshly washed wool, gleaming pile luster under open air drying.

---

### 5. Stage 05: PACKING & DISPATCH
- **Video File Name**: `mfg_05_packing.mp4`
- **Thumbnail File Name**: `mfg_05_packing.jpg`

  - **Scene**: Quality control inspection room and warehouse dispatch area.
  - **Focal Action**: Quality control expert measuring knot density with a magnifying loupe, tightly rolling completed rugs in protective moisture-proof jute/biodegradable covers, affixing export labels and brand tags for worldwide shipping.
  - **Lighting & Mood**: Clean, organized warehouse environment showcasing finished luxury rugs ready for international shipment.
